# Writer brief: Trump says Israel's $4 billion a year ends by New Year's

*fact_check · 2026-11-03. Written by Claude in an unattended scheduled run, not by `npm run draft` (no API key in that environment).*

**Angle:** Netanyahu has talked for months about weaning Israel off U.S. aid over about ten years. On Election Day morning, Trump told Fox & Friends it's over by the end of this year, and named Abdul El-Sayed as a reason. The deal that pays the money runs to September 2028, and Congress votes the money every year. Nobody has said how it ends early.

**Who's watching:** Gen Z, left-leaning. Many will cheer "aid to Israel is over". Don't give them that: Israel can still buy U.S. weapons, the aid was mostly spent on U.S. weapons anyway, and nothing official has changed yet.

**Feeling to leave them with:** "Wait, so did anything actually change?"

**Why they care:** It's the biggest U.S. aid relationship there is, and it's been a fight in their own elections.

**Funniest true detail:** His own Fox hosts asked whether he meant weapons or a check. "Any way you want to define it." And Israel's defense establishment reportedly learned about it from the interview.

## What's proven, alleged and denied
- **Proven, on the record (Fox & Friends, Nov. 3):** Trump said "by the end of this year we won't be paying Israel" (Reuters, France 24, Jerusalem Post transcript), "they don't need it" (everyone), called it a "bad talking point" and named El-Sayed, "lunatics like (an) El-Sayed" (CNN, The Hill, Reuters, NY Post, Breitbart, JPost). Asked weapons or a check: "(In/I'm giving four in) any way you want to define it" (CNN, Breitbart, JPost; the opening words differ, so only the shared phrase is quoted). Israel "can buy whatever they want" (Reuters, NY Post, JPost).
- **Trump's claim, unconfirmed:** that Netanyahu brought it up, and that they agreed "months ago". Netanyahu's office didn't comment (Reuters, CNN). JPost: the timeline "has not yet been independently confirmed by the Israeli government".
- **Proven, primary:** The 2016 MOU covers FY2019–FY2028, $38 billion, "subject to congressional appropriation" (CRS RL33222). Reuters: runs until September 2028, and the House and Senate pass appropriations every year. Israel is the largest cumulative recipient of U.S. foreign aid since World War II (CRS). Its F-35s were bought with U.S. aid (CRS).
- **Netanyahu's own position:** May, CBS 60 Minutes: draw down to zero "over the next decade" (The Hill, JPost); January, The Economist: within a decade (NY Post, JPost).
- **Reported reaction:** JPost: one IDF source "expressed surprise"; the defense, diplomatic and finance establishments "appear to have been caught by surprise". CNN: Eisenkot (Netanyahu's rival) said the defense establishment is learning about it "through the media".
- **Poll:** Quinnipiac, June: 48% of voters say the U.S. is too supportive of Israel (Reuters, France 24).
- **Complicates the left's cheer:** Israel keeps buying U.S. weapons; the aid largely ends up with U.S. arms makers (CNN); nothing official yet.
- **Complicates Trump:** No mechanism given (Reuters); Congress funds it; he tied it to a Democrat on Election Day.
