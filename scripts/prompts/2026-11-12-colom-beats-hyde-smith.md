# Writer brief: Scott Colom beats Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi (2026-11-12)

Written by Claude in an unattended scheduled run (no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, so not `npm run draft`). Modeled on the Two Dan Sullivans brief.

## Story

On Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, the AP called Mississippi's Senate race for Democrat Scott Colom, a district attorney, over Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith, 49.2% to 48.7%, by fewer than 5,000 votes. Years earlier, Biden nominated Colom to a federal judgeship and Hyde-Smith blocked it. On election night she told supporters she'd blocked him from being a judge and that they'd blocked him from the Senate. Then she lost.

## Why this one

Fresh (called Nov. 11, covered L/C/R), a recognizable-enough opener (a sitting senator, official portrait), a revenge arc anyone can follow in a minute, and an absurd true detail: a cattle farmer answering a question about beef prices with "so many proteins to choose from". Different from the last four videos (Trump/Dana White, Huang, Hegseth/Massie, Jordan): new person, new state, a race result rather than a scandal.

## Proven (on the record, two or more outlets)

- AP called it Wednesday, Nov. 11; Colom 49.2%, Hyde-Smith 48.7% (Washington Examiner, NY Post, BBC). Fewer than 5,000 votes apart (NY Post, NPR, BBC: "out of nearly a million counted").
- First Democrat to win a Mississippi Senate race since 1982 (Examiner, NY Post, Guardian, NPR).
- First Black senator from Mississippi since Reconstruction; Blanche Bruce served 1875-1881 (BBC; NY Post, NPR, Guardian, Mother Jones).
- Trump won Mississippi by more than 22 points in 2024 (BBC; NY Post 22.9; Mother Jones 23).
- Hyde-Smith in the Senate since 2018 (BBC; NY Post "first appointed in 2018").
- Biden nominated Colom to a federal district judgeship; Hyde-Smith blocked it (Examiner: blue slip; NPR; Guardian; Mother Jones). Wicker indicated support (NPR; Guardian "Republicans, including the senator Roger Wicker ... supported").
- Her stated reasons: Soros money to a PAC around his DA run, and his stance on legislation about trans athletes (NPR, Examiner). Colom said the PAC was independent, not his campaign (NPR).
- Election night: "I blocked Scott Colom from being a federal judge, and you blocked him from being [a] U.S. senator." (NPR "election night ... telling her supporters"; Guardian "on election day". First clause identical in both; second clause differs by "a", so the script paraphrases it.)
- Hyde-Smith, a cattle farmer, on beef prices: "You know, you have so many proteins to choose from" (Guardian, full); consumers "have so many proteins to choose from" (Examiner). NPR: WLOX interview, "pick a different protein".
- Colom's ad: man at a grill, "Pick another protein?" ... "pick another senator instead" (Guardian and Examiner, both phrases verbatim). It went viral (BBC, Examiner, Guardian).
- National Democrats came late: Guardian (party money only a month out), NPR (attention and funds "came late"); Schumer-aligned PAC put in $1.2M about two weeks before (NPR, Examiner).
- Hyde-Smith conceded: "I respect the decision of Mississippi voters." (NY Post, BBC; lead-in wording differs, this clause identical.)

## Alleged / disputed

- Nothing central is alleged. Her reasons for the block are stated as hers; Colom's rebuttal noted in the brief, not the script (time).
- Nomination year differs (Examiner 2022, Guardian/NPR 2023), so the script gives no year.

## What complicates each side

- Against our lean: the margin is under 5,000 votes out of nearly a million; national Democrats barely helped until the end; Hyde-Smith had stated policy reasons for the block and conceded graciously.
- Against Republicans: she bragged about the block on election night, then lost a state Trump won by 22.
