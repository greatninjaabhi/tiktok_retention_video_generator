# Writer brief: Trump offers Dana White the press secretary job, then posts a cartoon of fighters beating reporters

*explainer · 2026-11-08. Written by Claude in an unattended scheduled run, not by `npm run draft` (no API key in that environment).*

**Angle:** On Nov. 7, after the midterm losses, Trump posted that he'd offered the White House press secretary job to UFC boss Dana White, "a violent Fighter and Warrior", and asked him to bring six UFC fighters as "assistants" to "defend us against the Lunatic Left". A White House official says it was a joke, a CNN source says White said no, and White says he's meeting Trump on Monday, Nov. 9. On Nov. 8 Trump shared a cartoon of UFC fighters beating up White House reporters.

**Who's watching:** Gen Z, left-leaning, many of them UFC fans. Don't sneer at White or the fans: give him credit for helping Trump reach young men in 2024.

**Feeling to leave them with:** "The job is talking to reporters, and the pitch is fighters who tackle them."

**Why they care:** The press secretary is how the White House answers questions. The seat has been empty since mid-October, right as Democrats take Congress and promise investigations.

**Funniest true detail:** Asked how he'd fit the job in, White said "I got a lot of jobs already." He runs UFC, Power Slap and Zuffa Boxing, and sits on Meta's board.

## What's proven, alleged and denied
- **Proven, on the record (Trump on Truth Social, Nov. 7):** "the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary"; bring "assistants, even though some are not from our Country" (six named fighters); "We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left"; "Dana, please take the job!" (CNN, NPR, BBC and NY Post quote it the same way.)
- **White's answer:** an Instagram photo of himself at the briefing-room podium captioned "On to the next chapter" (NPR, BBC, HuffPost). At a London boxing news conference: "I got a lot of jobs already" (BBC; NPR: "Yeah, I got a lot of jobs already"; NY Post has "I have a lot of jobs already"). Meeting Trump Monday, Nov. 9 (NPR, BBC, NY Post, CNN, WaPo).
- **Steven Cheung**, White House communications director: "Hell yeah! Do it, @DanaWhite!" (BBC, CNN).
- **Is it real?** A White House official said the offer was a joke (WaPo, anonymous; CNN: "one aide said Trump was joking"). A source told CNN White declined (single anonymous source, CNN only; BBC and HuffPost repeat it citing CNN). White himself hasn't said yes or no as of Nov. 8.
- **The vacancy:** Karoline Leavitt left at the end of August. Katie Zacharia lasted five days in October (CNN, NPR, WaPo, NY Post; BBC: "less than five days").
- **The cartoon, Nov. 8:** about a minute long; White at the lectern says "Trump made me press secretary. I brought help"; fighters attack reporters; cartoon Trump asks "How was the briefing?" and White answers "Fiery … but mostly peaceful" (WaPo, TMZ; Washington Examiner describes the beating). Described as AI-generated by the Examiner and TMZ ("appears to be"), so the script just says "cartoon".
- **White's jobs:** UFC CEO, Power Slap, Zuffa Boxing, Meta board member since last year (BBC; NPR on Zuffa Boxing).
- **Complicates the left:** White really did help Trump reach young men in 2024 (CNN). And it may be a joke.
- **Complicates Trump:** young voters swung hard to Democrats on Nov. 3 (AP Voter Poll; CNN on young men).
