## Story

On Nov. 9, 2026, Rep. Thomas Massie (R-Ky.) introduced eight articles of impeachment against Defense ("War") Secretary Pete Hegseth as a privileged resolution. On Nov. 10, Hegseth held his first Pentagon press conference since May and called it "sabotage", "a farce" and "a stunt". A procedural House vote was set for 5:30 p.m. ET on Nov. 10.

## Proven (on the record, two or more outlets)

- Massie introduced eight articles on Nov. 9 (Guardian, NY Post, NPR, Hill). Three cite the Iran war / War Powers; others cover Yemen, the Maduro capture, civilian-casualty laws, "extrajudicial killings" on alleged drug boats, and Sen. Mark Kelly.
- Hegseth spoke for Massie's Trump-backed challenger Ed Gallrein in Hebron, Ky., on May 18, the day before the May 19 primary (WLOS/WKRC; NY Post calls it "unprecedented"). Massie lost (Guardian, NY Post). He leaves office when the new Congress is sworn in Jan. 3.
- Hegseth quotes, Nov. 10: "This is not patriotism. This is sabotage." (Guardian, NY Post, Mother Jones); "farce" / "stunt" (Guardian, The Hill); "disgruntled congressman" (The Hill, NY Post); "I stand behind every one of these operations. I was proud to execute them, and I would do it again tomorrow." (Washington Examiner, NY Post). Says every operation was lawful, ordered by Trump and notified to Congress (Guardian; Mother Jones: "every one was a lawful operation").
- House GOP majority 218-214 (Guardian). Conviction needs two-thirds / 67 senators (Guardian, NPR).
- Only two cabinet secretaries impeached before: Belknap (1876) and Mayorkas (2024) (NY Post, Guardian, Wikipedia).
- Mayorkas: Senate Democrats dismissed both articles on April 17, 2024, without a trial; Schumer said they failed "the high standard of high crimes and misdemeanors" (AP via The Journal; Wikipedia).

## Alleged / disputed

- That the Iran war and the other operations were illegal: Massie's allegation. Hegseth says all were lawful. Say "Massie alleges".
- Hegseth's claim Massie is driven by "ego and resentment": Hegseth's claim, not ours.

## What complicates each side

- Against Massie: he lost a primary in which Hegseth campaigned against him, so a grudge reading exists. Most Republicans are still expected to vote no.
- Against our lean: Democrats threw out the Mayorkas articles in 2024 as not rising to high crimes. Hegseth is making that kind of argument now (NY Post: impeachment isn't a remedy for policy disagreements).
