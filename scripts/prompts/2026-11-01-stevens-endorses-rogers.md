# Writer brief: Haley Stevens endorses Mike Rogers, and Trump calls it bravery

*fact_check · 2026-11-01. Written by Claude in an unattended scheduled run, not by `npm run draft` (no API key in that environment).*

**Angle:** Both sides of this feud have a point, and the endorsement is still hard to square with what she said about Rogers all year.

**Who's watching:** Gen Z, mostly not following the Senate map. Left-leaning viewers will want Stevens to be a simple villain. Don't give them that: she did get uninvited, and the ad fight is real.

**Feeling to leave them with:** "That is wild, and I get why it happened."

**Why they care:** It's one of the tightest Senate races in the country, two days before voting.

**Funniest true detail:** By Mother Jones' count she called Rogers a Trump "rubber stamp" at least 14 times. Trump, who's never met her, now says he'd love to have her.

## What's proven, alleged and denied
- **Proven, on the record:** Stevens endorsed Rogers on video and appeared with him at a Detroit church on Nov. 1. Trump's bravery post (quoted the same way by The Hill and the New York Post). Schumer's "despicable betrayal". Hertel's statement that she's not a member of the state party. Chief of staff Justin German's resignation and statement.
- **Reported through sources or her own account:** that El-Sayed disinvited her. The Hill cites sources, the BBC says "reportedly", the Post says "it was revealed". Stevens says so herself, and El-Sayed hasn't been read disputing it.
- **Disputed:** whether El-Sayed rejected the ad. On Saturday he told reporters "that ad was not made by me, and I do not agree with the contents of that ad" (CBS), while waving off "the condemn game". NPR and the BBC report the criticism. The Guardian says he declined her request. Jewish Insider, days earlier, said he distanced himself but hadn't explicitly condemned it.
- **Complicates Stevens:** months of attacks on Rogers. Staff quit. She'd said days earlier she'd vote for El-Sayed. She set conditions on the unity event before she was disinvited.
- **Complicates El-Sayed:** her fiancé and former campaign chair back Rogers. He campaigned with Hasan Piker, then distanced himself without disavowing. Jewish and Black voter concerns are reported by NPR and the New York Post.
- **Complicates Trump and the GOP:** Trump called El-Sayed a "jihadist" and "a Muslim Brotherhood guy" in the same exchange. Bridge Michigan and AP found no evidence of ties, and PolitiFact rated "didn't disavow" against the video. Left out of this script for length; the Oct. 31 video covers it.

- Saying the ad was antisemitic or wasn't. Say what it blamed.
- Saying the fiancé caused it. Newsom and others hint at that; nothing shows it.
