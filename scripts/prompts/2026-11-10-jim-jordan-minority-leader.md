# Writer brief: Jim Jordan elected House Republican leader (2026-11-10)

Written by Claude in an unattended scheduled run (no `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, so not `npm run draft`). Modeled on the Two Dan Sullivans brief.

## Story

On Tuesday, Nov. 10, 2026, House Republicans elected Rep. Jim Jordan (R-Ohio) as their leader for the next Congress (minority leader, since Democrats won the House on Nov. 3). He beat outgoing Majority Leader Steve Scalise 106-73 in a closed-door secret ballot. Speaker Mike Johnson didn't run for the job.

## Proven (on the record, two or more outlets)

- Jordan beat Scalise 106-73, secret ballot, Nov. 10 (The Hill, Washington Examiner; Guardian, NY Post confirm the win).
- Republicans lost the House; Democrats flipped more than 40 seats (NPR, NY Post). Jordan leads the minority from January (Guardian, NY Post, Examiner).
- Johnson announced he wouldn't seek the post (NY Post, Examiner, HuffPost, The Hill).
- Johnson on CNN, Nov. 9: "I'm sorry that the voters made the wrong choice." (NY Post and HuffPost, identical wording; Manu Raju video.)
- Jordan: 20-year House veteran, first elected 2006 (The Hill, AP via WHYY). Co-founder and first chairman of the Freedom Caucus (The Hill, NPR, AP).
- Freedom Caucus built its power attacking its own party's leaders; under its pressure Boehner resigned in 2015 (The Hill, WVXU).
- Boehner on Jordan, 2017 Politico Magazine interview: "A terrorist. A legislative terrorist." (The Week quoting Politico; WVXU: Boehner "later called Jordan a 'legislative terrorist'".) Jordan said he felt "sorry for the guy if he's that bitter" (The Week; search summaries).
- 2023: Jordan lost three House floor votes for Speaker in one week (Oct. 17-20); 20, 22, then 25 Republicans voted against him (AP via WHYY; Washington Examiner; The Hill "repeatedly fell short").
- Trump endorsed Jordan in 2023 (NPR, AP); stayed out of this race (NPR, NY Post).
- Jordan backed McCarthy even when conservatives ousted him in 2023 (The Hill; AP: McCarthy nominated Jordan on the floor).
- Jordan spent the last two years campaigning for GOP candidates (Washington Examiner, The Hill).
- Jordan's letter: "I am battle-tested on the oversight front" (NY Post, NPR, The Hill, Examiner). Democrats plan Trump investigations; Jordan is the first line of defense (The Hill, NPR).
- Jeffries is in line to be Speaker (The Hill, NY Post).
- Jordan is a former champion wrestler (The Hill) and wrestling coach (NPR, AP).

## Alleged / disputed

- Nothing central is alleged. "Legislative terrorist" is Boehner's insult, said as his.
