**Angle:** Ballot-stacking looks gross and may be true here, but "voter fraud at the highest level" is a much bigger claim than anyone has proven.

**Who's watching:** Gen Z, mostly not following the Senate map. They've seen "Decoy Dan" clips or heard Trump yelling about it and don't know what's real. Left-leaning viewers will want Democrats to be innocent; don't give them that for free.

**Feeling to leave them with:** "That is absurd, and I actually get what's proven and what isn't."

**Funniest true detail:** The FBI went to a boat dealership in a tiny fishing town to ask whether a $500 donor felt misled about which Dan he was supporting. He told AP he wasn't, and that "everybody knows who the incumbent is."

## Avoid
- Calling Daniel J. Sullivan "Decoy Dan" in our own voice. It's a label, not a finding, and he denies it.
- Saying Peltola knew or that she's lying. The manager's statement was retracted, and Peltola denies it.
- Saying "both sides are the same." One 2024 example doesn't prove that.
- Predicting who wins or implying the result will be invalid. Voting info only from vote.gov.
- Omitting the manager's attempted retraction, or that she was fired.
